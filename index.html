ATP CS-08 OFFLINE-FIRST EMERGENCY APP

Emergency coordination that still works when the internet goes down.

During floods and landslides the mobile network and power are often the first things to go. Trinode runs on a local Wi-Fi network at the relief camp, so people can send help requests from their phone browser and coordinators can see them, without any internet connection.

HOW IT WORKS

How it works

The basic flow from someone asking for help to a team reaching them.

01
1

Connect to Base

Join the Trinode-Emergency Wi-Fi at the camp. The app opens in the phone browser, so no install, SIM or data is needed.

2

Submit Request

Pick what you need (rescue, medical, food, water or shelter), set how urgent it is and add a nearby landmark.

3

Store & Forward

If you are out of range, the request is saved on the phone (IndexedDB) and sent when the phone is back in Wi-Fi range.

4

Coordinator Triage

Coordinators see new requests on the dashboard, check for duplicates and assign a team.

5

Rescue

The team goes to the location, marks the task as done and shelter numbers are updated.

WHO IT'S FOR

Who uses Trinode

The app has four user roles, each with its own set of screens.

03

Resident / Civilian

Send rescue, medical, food or water requests, follow their progress and find nearby shelters. No account needed.

Screens A1–A2 · R1–R13

Community Volunteer

Enter a name once and get simple tasks like handing out water or checking on elderly people. Can also report for neighbours.

Screens V1–V5

Rescue Team

Rescue teams sign in with a PIN, get their assigned tasks in order of urgency and record which shelter people were taken to.

Screens T1–T4

Camp Coordinator

Camp staff use the triage board to sort requests, merge duplicates, assign teams, manage shelter space and export CSV logs.

Screens D1–D11 · C1
